## Finding

Out of 9 client-trials, 7 first drafts broke at least one rule. After 3 rounds of revision, compliance was 33% with self-check, 22% with the bare verdict and 22% with the explained feedback. Explained feedback reached compliance in fewer rounds than bare in 0, the same in 9 and more in 0 client-trials (sign test p = 1.000); versus self-check: 0 / 8 / 1 (p = 1.000).
