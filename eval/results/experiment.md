# The feedback experiment

12 client-trials; 10 first drafts broke ≥1 rule. 95% bootstrap CIs in brackets.

| Condition | Round 0 | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Capital moved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Self-check (compliance) | 17% [0, 42] | 25% [8, 50] | 33% [8, 58] | 33% [8, 58] | 12% [8, 17] |
| B. Bare verdict (compliance) | 17% [0, 42] | 25% [0, 50] | 33% [8, 58] | 42% [17, 67] | 12% [8, 16] |
| C. Explained (compliance) | 17% [0, 42] | 33% [8, 58] | 42% [17, 67] | 50% [25, 75] | 11% [7, 14] |

| Condition | Round 0 | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Self-check | 0.83 broken, 0.2 pp | 0.75 broken, 2.4 pp | 0.67 broken, 0.2 pp | 0.67 broken, 3.6 pp |
| B. Bare verdict | 0.83 broken, 0.2 pp | 0.75 broken, 2.9 pp | 0.67 broken, 0.1 pp | 0.58 broken, 0.1 pp |
| C. Explained | 0.83 broken, 0.2 pp | 0.67 broken, 0.2 pp | 0.58 broken, 0.1 pp | 0.50 broken, 0.1 pp |

**C vs B** (rounds to compliance): C fewer 2, same 9, more 1; sign test p = 1.000

**C vs A** (rounds to compliance): C fewer 3, same 8, more 1; sign test p = 0.625

## Finding

Out of 12 client-trials, 10 first drafts broke at least one rule. After 3 rounds of revision, compliance was 33% with self-check, 42% with the bare verdict and 50% with the explained feedback. Explained feedback reached compliance in fewer rounds than bare in 2, the same in 9 and more in 1 client-trials (sign test p = 1.000); versus self-check: 3 / 8 / 1 (p = 0.625).
